About

A race you can feel

Most racing games script their opponents. In XR-Drive the opponents learn. Each AI car has been trained to find its own racing line, defend a position and make an overtake, so no two races play out the same way.

The cockpit view puts you inside the car, with the track, the other cars and the dashboard in front of you. It is built as an Unreal Engine project and made for immersive hardware as well as the screen.

Reinforcement learning

How the AI learns to race

Reinforcement learning teaches by practice. The AI driver, called the agent, tries things on the track and gets feedback on how well they worked. Nobody tells it how to drive.

What learning looks like

Early on the car crashes and loses time. Over many thousands of attempts the results climb, then level off as the driving gets smooth and consistent.

From the first lap to a real opponent

  1. Learn to stay on track

    The agent starts with no knowledge. It is rewarded for moving forward and staying on the circuit, and it learns the basics of steering, throttle and braking.

  2. Learn to go fast

    Rewards shift toward speed and clean laps. The AI finds racing lines, brakes later and carries more speed through corners.

  3. Learn to race other cars

    With other cars on the track, the AI learns to follow, defend and overtake without contact.

  4. Drive against you

    The trained driver joins the simulation as an opponent, and you race it in the cockpit.

Physics

Why real physics matters

An AI can only learn skills that carry over to the track it drives on. Because XR-Drive simulates real vehicle physics, the AI has to deal with the same limits you do: tyres that run out of grip, weight that shifts under braking and momentum that is hard to shed.

That is why the cars feel believable. They are not following a recording. They are driving, and they make the same trade-offs a human driver would.

FAQ

Questions

What is reinforcement learning?

It is a way of training AI through trial and error. The AI takes actions, receives a reward when they work, and gradually learns which actions lead to the best results.

Are the AI cars scripted?

No. Their driving comes from a trained model, not a fixed path, so they react to what happens on the track.

Who makes XR-Drive?

XR-Drive is built by XR Dynamics Lab as an Unreal Engine project.
