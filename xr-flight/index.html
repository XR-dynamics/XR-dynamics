Flight dynamics

Real forces, not an animation.

The aircraft is flown by a physics-based flight dynamics model running in Chaos. Lift, drag and thrust act on the airframe every frame, so what you feel in the headset comes from the simulation, not from a scripted path.

01

Lift

Airflow over the wings produces the force that carries the aircraft.

02

Drag

Resistance builds with speed and attitude, and you feel it in how the jet responds.

03

Thrust

The throttle in your hand sets the engine thrust that drives the jet forward.

04

Fly-by-wire

Stick and throttle inputs pass through a control layer, as in a modern jet.

Cockpit · hand tracking

The cockpit Immersive XR

You see your own hands on the panel and reach for the controls, as you would in the real aircraft.

What you see

A full cockpit around you, with the flight display, panels and your hands in the scene.

How it works

Your hands are tracked by the headset and drive the stick and throttle directly. There is no gamepad in between.

Why it matters

Reaching for a control instead of pressing a button makes the cockpit feel like a place you are in.

Chase view · live telemetry

The flight model Physics

The jet flies on simulated lift, drag and thrust, with speed and altitude readouts on screen.

What you see

The aircraft banking over terrain, with airspeed and altitude values shown at the edges of the frame.

How it works

A flight dynamics model in Unreal Engine 5 with Chaos physics computes the forces on the airframe each frame.

Why it matters

Because the motion is simulated, the same model can be tested, tuned and reused in other aerospace scenarios.

Attitude · control response

Climb and attitude Control

Pitch, roll and throttle changes show up in the aircraft as you make them.

What you see

The jet changing attitude against the clouds and ground below, with the readouts updating as it moves.

How it works

Your stick and throttle inputs go through a fly-by-wire control layer before they reach the flight model.

Why it matters

It is the loop a pilot trains: input, response, correction. In VR you do it with your hands.

Runway · wind and weather

Against the wind Environment

Fly against the wind: the aircraft starts on a wet runway and the environment pushes back.

What you see

The F-35 lined up on a rain-soaked runway, seen from above, before the takeoff run.

How it works

Weather is part of the scene, and the flight model is built so that conditions can act on the aircraft.

Why it matters

Testing in difficult conditions is where simulation pays off, because you can repeat them on demand.

Flight Dynamics plugin · Unreal Engine

Flight Dynamics plugin Aerospace

The physics-based flight model behind XR-Flight, for Unreal Engine 5.5 to 5.8: real lift, drag and thrust simulated in Chaos.

Across the envelope

Realistic physics across the whole speed and altitude range, from runway take-off to landing, with retractable landing gear and an afterburner plume.

Fly-by-wire

A fly-by-wire layer keeps the aircraft inside its flight envelope while the control surfaces follow the flight computer.

Instruments

A HUD with airspeed, Mach, G, angle of attack, altitude, vertical speed and thrust, plus three camera modes.
